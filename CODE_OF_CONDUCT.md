# Code of conduct

## Commitment and scope

Everyone should be able to participate in RentnerProxy Marketplace without harassment, regardless of background, identity, ability, or experience. This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and empathy, including when disagreeing.
- Give constructive feedback about work and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect privacy, consent, and different levels of technical experience.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help restore a productive conversation.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexualized attention, trolling, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. Critique ideas and implementation decisions without attacking people.

## Reporting and responsibility

[@RentnerKev](https://github.com/RentnerKev), the project maintainer, handles moderation. For public conduct in an issue or pull request, you may ask `@RentnerKev` to moderate without repeating abusive content or posting personal details.

Public issues are not confidential. For a private report, use an existing private conversation with the maintainer if you have one; otherwise request a private contact method without describing the incident publicly. There is no dedicated private conduct-reporting address, independent enforcement team, or independent appeal reviewer. GitHub's own reporting tools are available for abuse on GitHub, including concerns involving the maintainer. Report technical vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer should review reports fairly, respect reporter privacy, and explain moderation decisions when appropriate. Contributions or comments that violate this policy may be edited, removed, or rejected.

## Enforcement

Responses depend on the impact and pattern of behavior:

1. **Correction:** explain the problem and request a change in behavior.
2. **Warning:** specify consequences and boundaries for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction:** suspend participation for serious or repeated violations.
4. **Permanent restriction:** exclude participants for sustained harassment, threats, or repeated serious violations.
